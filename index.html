LLM interpretability / explainability / safety

Siddarth Mamidanna

I am a computer science undergraduate at UC Santa Cruz working on mechanistic interpretability, model explainability, and monitoring for large language models.

Working with Leilani Gilpin, Yilun Zhou, and Ziyu Yao.

email cv google scholar x

[01]

Work

I study how reasoning behavior emerges inside model computation, with a focus on mechanistic interpretability and the movement of information across tokens.

I am also interested in explanation quality: when model-generated explanations reflect real computation, when they do not, and what kinds of evidence make that distinction clearer.

More recently, I have been thinking about monitoring reasoning traces and outputs in practical settings, especially where interpretability methods might help catch failures earlier.

Siddarth Mamidanna, Daking Rai, Ziyu Yao, Yilun Zhou

EMNLP, 2025
